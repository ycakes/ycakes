Replaced with an `Intl.DateTimeFormat({ timeZone: "Africa/Cairo" }).formatToParts()` + `Date.UTC()` calculation, which measures the offset explicitly and doesn't depend on the host's default timezone at all — verified with `TZ=Africa/Cairo` vs. unset to confirm both environments now produce the same, correct result.
